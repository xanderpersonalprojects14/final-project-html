Career path

Software Developer

Out of all the paths in the computing industry, I'm most interested in becoming a Software Developer. I like the idea of turning an idea or a problem into something people can actually use — an app, a website, or a tool that makes someone's day a little easier.

Lesson 1 showed me that computing careers aren't one-size-fits-all; development is just one path among many, alongside networking, security, and data.

Why development?
  • Build things people can actually use
  • Solve real problems with code
  • Keep learning as technology changes
